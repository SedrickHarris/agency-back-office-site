How the training works

Every topic follows the same four-part path, from understanding a tactic to running it as an ongoing discipline. The examples below use the review-request workflow from the top of this page.

The four stages of training and what each one produces
StageWhat you doWhat you leave with
1Learn the work Understand what a tactic is, why it matters, and how it works.Example: what a review request is, and why the timing of the ask matters. Plain-language definitions and a clear picture of how the tactic works.
2Build the system Turn that knowledge into a repeatable process your team can follow.Example: the five-step workflow, from a finished job to a published reply. A repeatable process, written down so your team can follow it.
3Apply it to clients Connect the process to client delivery, outcomes, and retention.Example: set the workflow up in a client's system and adjust the message to their business. A client-ready version of the process, connected to delivery and retention.
4Keep improving Treat SEO, AEO, reputation, content, and automation as ongoing work, not one-time tasks.Example: check which requests get responses, then adjust the message and timing. A review routine for refining the work over time.

Stage 4 leads back to stage 1: improvement feeds the next round of learning.

Core topics, defined

Last reviewed: October 25, 2026

Image placeholder21:9 -SEO diagram

SEO

Search engine optimization (SEO) is the practice of improving a website so search engines can find it, understand it, and show it to people searching for related topics. It covers technical setup, content, site structure, and links. Agencies use SEO to help clients earn more qualified organic visits without paying for each click.

SEO training for agencies→
Image placeholder21:9 -AEO diagram

AEO

Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring content so search features and AI assistants can pull a clear, accurate answer from it. That means direct answers, defined terms, logical steps, and consistent naming. Agencies use AEO alongside SEO. No method guarantees a page will be quoted or cited.

AEO training→
Image placeholder21:9 -GBP diagram

GBP

A Google Business Profile (GBP) is the free listing a business manages to control how it appears in Google Search and Maps. It holds details like categories, services, hours, photos, and reviews. Agencies that manage listings for clients use GBP training to learn setup, optimization, and ongoing maintenance.

Google Business Profile training→
Image placeholder21:9 -Local SEO diagram

Local SEO

Local SEO is search engine optimization focused on helping a business appear when people search for services in a specific area. It combines Google Business Profile management, location and service content, directory listings, reviews, and local links. Agencies use it to serve clients that depend on customers in their area.

Frequently asked questions

About the training

What is Agency Back Office?

Agency Back Office is an online training company that teaches agency owners, marketers, and consultants the practical systems behind digital marketing and agency operations. Training focuses on implementation: workflows, examples, templates, and checklists you can apply to client work.

Who is the training for?

It is for people who sell or deliver digital marketing services: agency owners, new agency owners, SEO and web design agencies, AI automation and GoHighLevel agencies, marketing consultants, and freelancers. The audience pages show which topics fit each situation.

Where should I start if I'm new?

Start with Digital Marketing Training, which shows how the main channels fit together in client work, then choose the topic closest to the service you sell or want to add. The new agency owners page explains what to learn next.

Is the training online?

Yes. Agency Back Office is an online-only business, and training is delivered online. The current formats are live training and sessions.

Topics and expectations

What topics does Agency Back Office teach?

Fifteen topics, including SEO, AEO, Local SEO, Google Business Profile, reputation management, AI marketing, marketing automation, lead generation, and agency operations. The services page lists every topic.

What is the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO helps search engines find, understand, and show a website. AEO structures content so search features and AI assistants can pull a clear answer from it. The two overlap, and agencies use them together. See SEO training and AEO training.

How long does SEO take to work?

It depends on the site, the competition, and where the site starts, and no one can promise a timeline. Search engines decide when and how results change, so honest timelines are estimates, not promises. Tracking progress over time is more reliable than expecting a fixed date.

Can training guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No. No training or tactic can guarantee rankings, featured snippets, AI answer inclusion, or knowledge panels, because search engines and AI systems make those decisions. Agency Back Office teaches the practices that improve how well a site can be found and understood.
