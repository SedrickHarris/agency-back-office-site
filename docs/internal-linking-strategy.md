# Internal Linking Strategy

## Objective

Create explicit topical relationships between services, audiences, training, and resources.

## Page-Level Requirements

### Service Pages Link To

- Services hub
- related service pages
- relevant audiences
- approved audience + service pages
- guides
- FAQs
- checklists/templates
- training opportunities
- CTA pages

### Audience Pages Link To

- Audiences hub
- relevant service pages
- audience + service pages
- related resources
- training
- CTA pages

### Audience + Service Pages Link To

- parent audience
- canonical service
- related service combinations
- relevant resources
- training
- CTA

### Resources Link To

- parent resource hub
- canonical service/topic page
- related resources
- relevant audience where appropriate
- next-step CTA

## Anchor Text

Use descriptive anchors that communicate the destination.

Prefer:
`SEO training for agency owners`

Avoid:
`click here`

## Link Placement

Use:
- contextual body links
- related-topic modules
- breadcrumb links
- hub navigation
- next-step links

## Orphan Prevention

No indexable content page should launch without at least one intentional internal link pointing to it.

## Authority Flow

High-level hubs should link downward to important children.

Supporting resources should link upward to canonical service pages.

Sibling pages should link laterally where the relationship genuinely helps the user.
