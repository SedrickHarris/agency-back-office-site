## Governance

A new service may be added only after:

1. confirming it is a meaningful Agency Back Office training area,
2. checking for overlap with an existing service,
3. defining search intent,
4. assigning a canonical slug,
5. updating the page list, matrix, internal linking, and topic map.

Do not create new service routes ad hoc during page implementation.
