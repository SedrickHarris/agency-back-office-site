# Project Overview

## Project

Agency Back Office Website Growth System

## Purpose

Create a scalable educational and customer-acquisition website for Agency Back Office.

Agency Back Office trains agency owners, marketers, consultants, and freelancers in practical digital marketing and agency implementation disciplines.

## Core Training Topics

1. Digital Marketing Training
2. SEO Training
3. AEO Training
4. Google Business Profile Training
5. Reputation Management Training
6. Local SEO Training
7. Website SEO Training
8. Content Marketing Training
9. AI Marketing Training
10. Marketing Automation Training
11. Lead Generation Training
12. Conversion Optimization Training
13. Website Strategy Training
14. Agency Operations Training
15. CRM Training

## Primary Audiences

- Agency owners

## Primary Site Architecture

The site will use a hub-and-spoke structure:

- Core pages
- Service/topic pages
- Audience pages
- Audience + service pages
- Training delivery pages
- Resource clusters
- Conversion pages

## Search Objectives

The website should support visibility across:

- Google Search
- Bing
- Featured snippets
- People Also Ask
- Voice search
- Answer engines
- ChatGPT
- Gemini
- Perplexity
- Other LLM-based discovery systems

Local search is a taught subject, not a ranking channel for Agency Back Office itself.

## Conversion Objectives

Primary visitor actions may include:

- Explore training
- Join Agency Back Office
- View courses
- Attend training
- Access a guide/checklist/template
- Contact Agency Back Office
- Get started

## Project Principles

- Build for people first.
- Use search optimization to improve discoverability, not to create thin pages.
- Treat content architecture as a system.
- Use documentation as the source of truth.
- Prefer reusable components and structured content.
- Preserve stable URLs.
- Avoid fabricated claims.
- Optimize for performance, accessibility, comprehension, and conversion.
