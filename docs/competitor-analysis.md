# Competitor Analysis Framework

## Purpose

Identify how Agency Back Office can create stronger educational architecture, content depth, positioning, and conversion than competing training platforms and educators.

## Competitor Categories

Research should include:

- agency education platforms
- digital marketing training programs
- SEO educators
- local SEO educators
- GBP training providers
- reputation management educators
- agency coaching programs
- AI marketing/automation training platforms

## Analysis Dimensions

For each competitor, capture:

- brand positioning
- primary audiences
- core offers
- service/topic coverage
- course structure
- site architecture
- URL strategy
- resource strategy
- organic visibility
- content depth
- topical gaps
- audience targeting
- internal linking
- conversion architecture
- proof/trust signals
- schema use
- design patterns

## Opportunity Questions

- Which important topics are underserved?
- Which audiences are poorly addressed?
- Which training concepts are explained poorly?
- Where is content too theoretical?
- Where can Agency Back Office provide implementation frameworks?
- Which resource types are missing?
- Which questions have weak current answers?

## Rule

Competitor research informs strategy. Do not copy competitor copy, branding, page structure, or proprietary material.
