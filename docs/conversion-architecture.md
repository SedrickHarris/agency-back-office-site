# Conversion Architecture

## Objective

Move visitors from education to the most appropriate next action without forcing every page into the same funnel.

## Conversion Levels

### Low Intent

Examples:
- glossary
- blog
- introductory FAQ

CTAs:
- read a related guide
- download a checklist
- explore training
- subscribe if available

### Medium Intent

Examples:
- service page
- audience page
- guide

CTAs:
- view relevant training
- explore courses
- see how Agency Back Office helps
- get started

### High Intent

Examples:
- training offer
- enrollment page
- get started
- contact

CTAs:
- join
- enroll
- register
- contact
- start training

## Contact vs Get started

| Page | Job | Visitor |
|---|---|---|
| `/contact/` | Questions and inquiries about training | Not sure yet, or wants to ask something |
| `/get-started/` | Enroll or membership path once terms are confirmed | Knows they want training |

Cross-link rule:
- Contact links to Get started as a secondary text link, never a button.
- Get started links to Contact as a text link ("Have a question first? Contact us").
- Each page keeps one dominant CTA. The two never appear as competing buttons in one viewport.
- Contact is not in the primary navigation. It lives in the footer.

## CTA Rules

- Match CTA to page intent.
- Use one dominant CTA and optional secondary CTA.
- Avoid competing actions in the same viewport.
- Keep CTA copy specific.
- Repeat the primary CTA after major decision sections where appropriate.

## Trust Elements

Use only real, verified proof such as:
- instructor background
- training examples
- curriculum
- screenshots
- testimonials
- community proof
- outcomes
- case studies

Do not fabricate any proof.

## Conversion Content

High-intent pages should clarify:
- what the visitor gets
- who it is for
- what they will learn
- delivery format
- expected next step
- objections/FAQs
- any real pricing or terms once confirmed

## Measurement

Track:
- CTA clicks
- form starts/completions
- enrollment clicks
- resource downloads
- training page views
- conversion rate by landing page
