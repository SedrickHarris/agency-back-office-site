# Target Audience

## Audience Strategy

Agency Back Office serves people responsible for delivering or selling digital marketing services. Audience pages should be built around actual operating needs, not demographic assumptions.

## Primary Audiences

### Agency Owners

Needs:
- better service delivery systems
- profitable fulfillment
- client retention
- repeatable processes
- clearer offers
- improved technical knowledge

### New Agency Owners

Needs:
- foundational training
- what to sell
- how to fulfill services
- tools and workflows
- client onboarding
- practical implementation confidence

### Digital Marketing Agencies

Needs:
- broader service capability
- SEO/AEO integration
- local search
- reputation systems
- automation
- better website strategy

### SEO Agencies

Needs:
- AEO and AI-search evolution
- local SEO
- GBP
- technical SEO
- structured content systems
- entity and schema strategies

### Web Design Agencies

Needs:
- SEO-informed architecture
- content strategy
- conversion
- local search
- structured data
- ongoing services after launch

### AI Automation Agencies

Needs:
- demand generation
- local search integration
- website strategy
- CRM
- reputation workflows
- ways to combine AI automation with marketing outcomes

### GoHighLevel Agencies

Needs:
- CRM systems
- reputation management
- automation
- lead capture
- client onboarding
- SEO/GBP services that complement platform implementation

### Marketing Consultants

Needs:
- frameworks
- diagnostics
- execution support
- practical tools
- strategic integration

### Freelance Marketers and Web Designers

Needs:
- broader capability
- clearer service packaging
- implementation guidance
- SEO/AEO/GBP knowledge
- recurring service opportunities

## Audience Page Rule

Every audience page should answer:

1. What does this audience struggle with?
2. What outcomes do they need?
3. Which Agency Back Office training topics are most relevant?
4. What mistakes are common for this audience?
5. What should they learn next?
6. Which resources and service pages support that journey?

Audience pages must not be generic copies with only the audience noun changed.
