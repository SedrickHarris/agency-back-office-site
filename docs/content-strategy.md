# Content Strategy

## Objective

Create an interconnected educational ecosystem rather than a collection of isolated blog posts.

## Content Layers

### Layer 1 — Core Service Pages

Own major training topics.

### Layer 2 — Audience Pages

Explain Agency Back Office relevance to specific practitioner groups.

### Layer 3 — Audience + Service Pages

Connect a training topic to the specific needs of an audience.

### Layer 4 — Resource Hubs

Guides, FAQs, checklists, templates, glossary, blog.

### Layer 5 — Freshness Content

Platform updates, tactical changes, industry developments, new workflows.

## Resource Types

### Guides
Long-form implementation content.

### FAQs
Direct question-and-answer content.

### Checklists
Execution-focused resources.

### Templates
Reusable operational or marketing assets.

### Glossary
Definitions and entity reinforcement.

### Blog
Timely commentary, tutorials, updates, examples, and supporting topic coverage.

## Content Selection Test

Before creating a resource, identify:

- target question
- search intent
- parent service/topic
- target audience
- unique value
- internal links
- CTA
- expected lifespan
- whether it requires ongoing updates

## Freshness

Classify content as:

- evergreen
- periodic refresh
- time-sensitive

Platform-specific content should be reviewed more frequently.

## Quality Standard

Content should:
- be practical
- explain implementation
- include examples where useful
- avoid filler
- avoid unsupported claims
- connect to related topics
- support conversion without becoming sales-heavy
