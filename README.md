# Agency Back Office Website

Repository for the Agency Back Office website rebuild and growth platform.

Agency Back Office provides practical training for agency owners, marketers, consultants, freelancers, and service providers in digital marketing, SEO, AEO, local SEO, Google Business Profile optimization, reputation management, AI marketing, automation, lead generation, website strategy, CRM, and agency operations.

## Project Objectives

Build a fast, scalable, content-led website that:

- Establishes Agency Back Office as an authority in digital marketing and agency education.
- Creates strong topical authority around SEO, AEO, GBP optimization, reputation management, AI marketing, automation, and agency operations.
- Supports service pages, audience pages, and audience + service pages.
- Performs well in Google, Bing, answer engines, AI search, and LLM-assisted discovery.
- Converts visitors into students, members, leads, and training customers.
- Provides a maintainable content and component system for long-term expansion.
